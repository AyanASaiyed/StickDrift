# Welcome and log-in pages

Scope: `web/src/pages/Welcome.tsx` (signed-out front door), `web/src/pages/AuthPage.tsx` (resident and care-team log in and sign up). Visitor mode: Persuade for the welcome page (the visitor chooses a door and acts); the log-in forms are Operate inside the same world.

Audience, job, action: residents of care homes (older adults at a laptop with a PS5 pad) and care-team staff, equal weight. Each must find their door in seconds and log in or create an account. Proof: real captures of the three playable games (`web/public/lobby/*.jpg`). No invented testimonials or numbers. Voice: warm and plain. Constraint: large, easy-to-read type, high contrast.

Chosen direction: an akari workshop. The user picked it from the dealt options. Memorable moment: the two doors are paper lanterns that switch on one after the other, and brighten from the inside when you hover or focus them.

Unresolved: none. The three games stay as the only content; mock games live on the resident home page.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A front door made of light, not a hero with two buttons. Each door is a lit paper volume you walk up to. It refuses the centred headline over two stacked cards.

OWN-WORLD: A dim evening room, deep aubergine ground. Washi lantern volumes (a tall folded column and a round moon) glow from inside in persimmon and rose-amber, with bamboo-rib banding bowing across the paper. Rib tan for structure lines, charcoal ink on the pale paper forms, one vermilion hanko stamp as the only active mark. Light humanist rounded gothic for display, Atkinson Hyperlegible for reading. Forms are paper and cord, never glass or gradients on text.

STORY: Stick Drift is games you play with two thumbs, and the care team can see how it went. The visitor understands there are two doors, picks theirs, and logs in. The game stills prove the games are real.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, the stamp mark and the headline at display scale, one supporting sentence. Right, two lanterns hanging on cords at different heights, 300px and larger: the resident column and the care-team moon, each printed with its name; the vermilion stamp button sits under each as the primary action, a plain text link for creating an account under that. Below the fold edge, a folded paper strip of three lit game stills.

FORM: Akari light sculpture (challenger, catalog id paper-folds-pleats-deployable-akari-light-sculpture, chosen by the user over the assigned radio-listings direction); seed:a06ad164d5556db1.
